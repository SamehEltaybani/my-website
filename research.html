Research

My research focuses on generating actionable evidence to improve healthcare delivery and patient outcomes. I employ mixed-methods approaches, integrating quantitative and qualitative data to address complex clinical and policy questions.

Copyright: Photo by Carlos Muza on Unsplash

Research Approach

My research is grounded in evidence-based practice and driven by the need to bridge the gap between complex medical data and actionable clinical insights. I use a combination of quantitative and qualitative methods to explore healthcare challenges from multiple perspectives.

I collaborate with interdisciplinary teams to design studies that are rigorous, reproducible, and relevant to real-world clinical settings. My work has been published in leading healthcare journals and presented at international conferences.

Copyright: Photo by Carlos Muza on Unsplash

Data-Driven Insights

I use advanced data visualization techniques to transform complex datasets into clear, actionable insights. My work includes dashboard development, interactive visualizations, and reproducible data analysis pipelines.

I am proficient in R, Python, and JavaScript for data analysis and visualization, and I prioritize open science practices to ensure reproducibility and transparency.

Research Philosophy

I believe that research should be driven by curiosity and a commitment to improving human health. Every dataset tells a story, and rigorous inquiry is the foundation of better health outcomes. My research is guided by the principles of integrity, transparency, and collaboration.

I prioritize reproducible science and open communication, ensuring that my findings are accessible to clinicians, policymakers, and the broader research community.

Research Projects

This project explores the use of predictive analytics to identify neonates at risk of adverse outcomes in the NICU. Using machine learning models and electronic health record data, we aim to develop early warning systems that support clinical decision-making.

  • Collaborators: Department of Neonatology, Data Science Unit
  • Funded by: National Research Grant 2024

A qualitative meta-synthesis exploring barriers and facilitators to health equity in underserved populations. This study synthesizes findings from over 50 qualitative studies to inform policy and practice.

  • Collaborators: Department of Public Health, Policy Unit
  • Funded by: Health Equity Foundation

Developing a clinical data dashboard for real-time visualization of patient data in the intensive care unit. The dashboard integrates data from multiple sources to support clinical decision-making and improve patient safety.

  • Collaborators: ICU, Clinical Informatics Team
  • Funded by: Hospital Innovation Fund
