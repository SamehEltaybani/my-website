    Value in Action

    My core values shape not only what I believe, but also how I work, teach, collaborate, and make decisions.

    Integrity

    I work with honesty, transparency, and respect for evidence, people, and professional responsibility.

    Image source →

    Integrity means making decisions that remain responsible and defensible, even when the easier option may be more convenient.

    What I do

    • Make evidence and reasoning transparent
    • Respect participants, colleagues, and collaborators
    • Acknowledge limitations and uncertainty

    What I avoid

    • Making unsupported claims
    • Hiding limitations or conflicts of interest
    • Treating shortcuts as a substitute for rigor

    Impact

    I focus on work that can improve understanding, strengthen practice, and contribute to meaningful outcomes.

    Image source →

    Impact means connecting ideas to real needs and ensuring that knowledge has a clear path toward practical value.

    What I do

    • Focus on questions with practical relevance
    • Translate findings into understandable insights
    • Consider how work may affect practice and outcomes

    What I avoid

    • Producing work without a meaningful purpose
    • Defining success through numbers alone
    • Offering solutions disconnected from context

    Inspiration

    I aim to create curiosity, confidence, and a sense of possibility through learning and collaboration.

    Image source →

    Inspiration means helping people see new possibilities, ask better questions, and feel capable of contributing to improvement.

    What I do

    • Encourage curiosity and constructive questions
    • Share knowledge generously and clearly
    • Create space for confidence and participation

    What I avoid

    • Using gatekeeping as a substitute for guidance
    • Reducing learning to one-way instruction
    • Discouraging questions or alternative perspectives
